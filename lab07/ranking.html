Ranking Red Velvet's post-hiatus Songs


Learning while having Fun

Following their group hiatus from 2019 to 2021, Red Velvet has had 7 comebacks: 5 mini-albums, 1 full-length album, and 1 Japanese full-length album. This table ranks all of the songs on their post-hiatus mini-albums into tiers based on my personal liking. This is my first table in html! Not only did I use dashed borders borders and cell shading to represent all of the Red Velvet members colors, but I also practiced proper accessibility techniques by adding captions, labels, and scope attributes to the table. Do you agree with my tier list? Let me know! You can access the Submission Form here!
